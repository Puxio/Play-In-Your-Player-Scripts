Play In My Player

One bookmark that turns an album or playlist page into an .xspf playlist for VLC, foobar2000, AIMP, PotPlayer, MPV and other players.

The bookmarklet of Play-In-Your-Player-Scripts on GitHub.

Play In My Player v{{VERSION}}

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

Install

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B (Cmd + Shift + B on Mac).
  2. Drag the orange button onto the bar. The bookmark is named with its version, so you can always see which one you have.
  3. Updating from an older version? Delete the old bookmark and drag the new one.

Use

Open an album or playlist page on a supported site and click the bookmark. The .xspf file downloads automatically: open it with your player. A small panel in the top right corner of the page shows the progress and, if something goes wrong, the reason.

The scripts for each site are loaded fresh from GitHub on every click, so fixes reach you without reinstalling. The bookmark itself only needs replacing when a notice in the page corner tells you a new version is out.

Supported sites
